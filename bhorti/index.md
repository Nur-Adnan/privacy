# Privacy policy

_Last updated: 2026-11-19_

Bhorti is a study app for the Bangladesh medical admission test. It has no user account, no
advertising, no analytics and no crash reporting.

## What the app stores, and where

Everything you do in the app stays in a database on your phone:

- your highlights, notes and bookmarks;
- your reading progress and read-aloud position;
- your practice answers, mock test results and settings;
- a random install number the app creates on first launch.

Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data in Android settings, deletes all of it.

## What leaves your phone

**In this version, nothing.** The app makes no network requests. The books, the question bank
and the mock tests all work offline.

**Read aloud** hands the sentence being read to the text-to-speech engine you chose in your
phone's settings (for example Google's Speech Services). That engine is part of your phone, not
of this app, and its own privacy policy applies.

## When AI features are switched on

AI summaries, the AI tutor and new AI practice questions will arrive in a later update. This
policy will be updated before then. They are designed to work like this:

- A summary or a new question set sends only the book, its version and the page numbers. It never
  sends book text or anything you wrote.
- The tutor sends the question you type, your earlier questions in that chat, and the page
  numbers it is about.
- Each request carries the random install number, used only to apply daily fair-use limits.
  The server keeps per-install and per-network request counters for at most two days.
- Requests go to our server, which asks OpenAI to write the answer. Your questions are passed to
  OpenAI and are not stored on our server.
- Our server logs only book and page ids, sizes and timings. It never logs your questions, your
  install number or your IP address.
- A summary or question set made for some pages is stored on the server and shared with every
  student who asks for the same pages. It holds no personal data.
- Reporting a wrong question sends the question's id and the reason you picked, and the report is
  stored.

## Children

The app is made for students preparing for the medical admission test after HSC. It does not
knowingly collect personal information from anyone. As described above, it collects none at all.

## Not an official app

Bhorti is not affiliated with the Directorate General of Health Services (DGHS), the
Directorate General of Medical Education (DGME) or any government body.
